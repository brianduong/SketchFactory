Short drawing tutorials

Simple Sketch publishes short, calm drawing tutorials. Every video takes one subject — an animal, an everyday object — and reduces it to the fewest strokes that still read as itself. No colour, no shading, no rush. Follow along on paper, a whiteboard, or a tablet.
